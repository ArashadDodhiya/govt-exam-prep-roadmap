### 🏛️ A1. ANCIENT INDIA

#### Indus Valley Civilization (IVC) — MUST KNOW FOR GPSC

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Period | 2600–1900 BCE (Mature phase) |
| Also called | Harappan Civilization (Harappa discovered first, 1921 by Daya Ram Sahni) |
| Mohenjo-daro | Discovered 1922 by R.D. Banerji. Sindh, Pakistan. "Mound of the Dead" |
| Script | Undeciphered. Boustrophedon style (right to left, then left to right) |
| Metal NOT known | Iron ❌ (used Copper, Bronze, Gold, Silver) |
| Animal NOT found | Horse ❌ (found: Bull, Elephant, Rhinoceros, Tiger) |
| Great Bath | Mohenjo-daro — largest brick structure |
| Granary | Mohenjo-daro (largest); Harappa also had granary |
| Drainage system | Most advanced in ancient world — covered drains |
| Religion | Mother Goddess worship, Pashupati (Proto-Shiva), Tree worship, No temples found |
| Decline | Floods, Aryan invasion theory, Climate change, Tectonic activity |

#### Vedic Period

| Aspect | Early Vedic (Rigvedic) 1500–1000 BCE | Later Vedic 1000–600 BCE |
|--------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Text | Rigveda (oldest, 10 Mandalas, 1028 Suktas) | Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda |
| River | Sapta Sindhu (Indus region) | Ganga-Yamuna Doab |
| Society | Tribal, pastoral, cattle-rearing | Settled agriculture, varna system rigid |
| Polity | Tribal chief (Rajan), Sabha & Samiti | King powerful, Sabha declined |
| Women | Respected, Apala, Ghosa, Lopamudra (women sages) | Status declined |
| Economy | Pastoral (cow = wealth, "Gavishti" = war for cows) | Agriculture, trade, coins absent |
| God | Indra (most mentioned — 250 hymns), Agni, Varuna | Prajapati (supreme), Vishnu & Rudra rose |
| Iron | Not known ❌ | Known ✅ (Krishna Ayas) |

**Kautilya's Arthashastra** = Treatise on statecraft, economy, military strategy. Written by Chanakya (Vishnugupta).

#### ⭐ Solanki (Chalukya) Dynasty (942–1244 CE) — THE GOLDEN AGE OF GUJARAT

> **This is the SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT topic for GPSC Prelims. 5–8 questions guaranteed.**

| Ruler | Period | Achievements |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Mularaja I** | 942–997 CE | Founded Solanki dynasty. Built Modhera's early structures. Defeated Arab invaders |
| Bhima I | 1022–1064 CE | Somnath destroyed by Mahmud of Ghazni (1026 CE) during his reign. Rebuilt it |
| **Siddhraj Jaysinh** | 1094–1143 CE | **Greatest Solanki ruler**. Built Sahasralinga Talav (1000-Shivling lake, Patan). Patron of scholars (Hemachandra). Expanded empire. Golden age of architecture |
| **Kumarpal** | 1143–1172 CE | Disciple of Hemachandra. Embraced Jainism. Built temples. Promoted vegetarianism |
| Ajaypal | After Kumarpal | Last notable ruler. Dynasty weakened |

**Key Cultural Achievements of Solanki Period:**

| Achievement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Rani ki Vav** | Built by Queen Udayamati in memory of Bhima I (~1063 CE). Patan. **UNESCO World Heritage Site (2014)**. On ₹100 note. Inverted temple, 7 levels, 500+ sculptures |
| **Modhera Sun Temple** | Built by Bhima I (~1026 CE). Mehsana. No worship today. Remarkable geometry — sunlight falls on equinox. 3 parts: Surya Kund, Sabha Mandap, Guda Mandap |
| **Somnath Temple** | One of 12 Jyotirlingas. Repeatedly destroyed and rebuilt. Current temple built after independence (1951, K.M. Munshi & Sardar Patel initiative) |
| **Acharya Hemachandra** | Greatest scholar of Solanki era. Jain monk. Wrote Siddhahemachandra (Sanskrit grammar), Dwayashrayakavya (history of Solankis), Yoga Shastra |
| **Literature** | Golden age of Sanskrit & Prakrit literature. Gujarat emerged as intellectual center |
| **Architecture** | Maru-Gurjara style (Gujarat's unique temple architecture). Intricate carvings, step-wells |

#### Key Topics & Quick Tricks

**Number Series**: Find the pattern (add, multiply, square, cube, alternate).

**Example**: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ? → Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, **12** → Answer: **42**

**Coding-Decoding**: Letter shifts. A=1, B=2... Z=26. Check +1, +2, −1, reverse.

**Blood Relations**: Draw family trees. Father's/Mother's brother = Uncle. Key: identify gender first.

**Direction & Distance**: Draw on paper. N-E-S-W. Pythagoras for shortest distance.

**Clocks**: Angle = |30H − 5.5M|. If > 180°, subtract from 360°.

**Calendars**: Odd days method. Leap year = 2 odd days. Ordinary = 1 odd day.

**Syllogism**: Use Venn diagrams. All A are B = A ⊂ B. Some A are B = A ∩ B ≠ ∅.

### 💰 B2. MONETARY POLICY — RBI

| Tool | Current Value (approx.) | Function |
|------|------------------------|----------|
| **Repo Rate** | ~6.5% (check latest) | Rate at which RBI lends to banks |
| **Reverse Repo** | ~3.35% | Rate at which RBI borrows from banks |

**Inflation Control:**
- To **reduce** inflation → **increase** Repo, CRR, SLR (makes money expensive)
- To **boost** growth → **decrease** Repo, CRR, SLR (makes money cheap)

### 💰 B3. FISCAL POLICY — BUDGET

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Revenue Receipt** | Income that doesn't create liability (Tax + Non-tax) |
| **Capital Receipt** | Income that creates liability or reduces assets (Loans, Disinvestment) |
| **Revenue Expenditure** | Spending that doesn't create assets (Salary, Interest, Subsidies) |
| **Capital Expenditure** | Spending that creates assets (Roads, Buildings, Machines) |
| **Fiscal Deficit** | Total Expenditure − Total Receipt (excluding borrowings) = Government's total borrowing |
| **Revenue Deficit** | Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipt |
| **Primary Deficit** | Fiscal Deficit − Interest Payments |
| **FRBM Act (2003)** | Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management — limits government borrowing |

**Taxation:**

| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| **Direct Tax** | Income Tax, Corporate Tax, Wealth Tax (abolished 2016) |
| **Indirect Tax** | **GST** (replaced Excise, VAT, Service Tax, etc. from July 1, 2017) |

**GST Structure:**

| Rate | Items |
|------|-------|
| 0% | Essential food grains, fresh fruits/vegetables |
| 5% | Basic necessities, packaged food |
| 12% | Computers, processed food |
| 18% | Most goods and services, electronics |
| 28% | Luxury items, cars, tobacco |

**GST Council**: Art. 279A. Chaired by Union Finance Minister. State FMs are members. Decides rates.

# 🎯 PRELIMS EXAM-DAY STRATEGY

### Time Management (Per Paper — 3 Hours = 180 Minutes)

| Phase | Time | Action |
|-------|------|--------|
| **First Pass** | 0–120 min | Go through ALL 200 questions. Answer ONLY those you're 90%+ sure about. Mark doubtful ones |
| **Second Pass** | 120–160 min | Revisit marked questions. Answer those you're 70%+ sure about |
| **Final Pass** | 160–180 min | Review answers. Leave questions where you'd be pure guessing |

### Attempt Strategy

| Confidence | Action |
|------------|--------|
| 100% sure | Attempt ✅ |
| 75–99% sure | Attempt ✅ |
| 50–75% sure | Attempt (eliminate 2 options first) ✅ |
| 25–50% sure | **SKIP** ❌ (negative marking will kill you) |
| Below 25% | **SKIP** ❌ |

### The Math of Negative Marking

| Scenario | Net Marks |
|----------|----------|
| 4 correct + 0 wrong | +4 |
| 4 correct + 3 wrong | +4 − 1 = **+3** |
| 4 correct + 4 wrong | +4 − 1.32 = **+2.68** |
| 4 correct + 8 wrong | +4 − 2.64 = **+1.36** |
| 4 correct + 12 wrong | +4 − 4 = **0** (12 wrong cancels 4 right!) |

> **BRUTAL FACT**: Every 3 wrong answers cancel 1 right answer. If you attempt 180 questions and get 40 wrong, you lose 13.2 marks = ~13 marks thrown away.
