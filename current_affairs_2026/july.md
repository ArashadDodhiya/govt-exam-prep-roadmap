## 5. Defence: Exercises & Missile Tests

- **Kusha Long-Range Surface-to-Air Missile (LRSAM)** — DRDO conducted the maiden flight-test on 23 July 2026 from Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Island, Odisha coast; successfully intercepted an electronic target simulating a high-speed, high-altitude aerial threat; engagement range exceeds 120 km; intended for the Indian Air Force to reduce reliance on imported systems (like the Indo-Israeli MR-SAM/LR-SAM currently in use, ~70 km range); Defence Minister Rajnath Singh called it a milestone possessed by "only a handful of nations" **[High]**
- **Kusha programme background** — Approved by the Ministry of Defence in September 2023; all missile, radar, and command-control elements developed indigenously by DRDO labs and industry partners **[Medium]**
- **Integrated Battle Groups (IBGs)** — First five IBGs operationalised by the Indian Army on 1 July 2026 (see Policies section above) **[High]**

## 6. Science & Technology

- **Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-1 — India's first private orbital rocket** — Launched 18 July 2026 from Sriharikota, reached ~450 km low Earth orbit in 17 minutes; carried 4 customer payloads (including SCOPE and Grahaa satellites); made India the THIRD country (after USA and China) where a private company — not a government agency — has achieved orbital launch **[High]**
- **Skyroot background** — Hyderabad-based startup founded 2018 by ex-ISRO engineers Pawan Kumar Chandana and Naga Bharath Daka; previously launched India's first-ever private rocket, the suborbital Vikram-S, in November 2022; became India's first space-tech unicorn in May 2026 after raising $60 million (valuation ~$1.1 billion) **[Medium]**
- **India's private space sector scale** — Grew from ~1 registered space startup in 2014 to 400+ by early 2026, with cumulative sector investment crossing $500 million **[Medium]**
- **ISRO's 2026 launch cadence** — ISRO Chairman V. Narayanan announced a target of 7 launches for the current financial year, including a ~6,500 kg US-built commercial communication satellite — its busiest launch calendar in history **[Medium]**
- **Gaganyaan progress** — ISRO completed three major qualification tests for the crew module in July 2026 (flotation unit for post-splashdown righting, and crew-service module umbilical disconnect mechanism); no firm crewed-flight date announced; uncrewed orbital test flight (carrying humanoid robot "Vyommitra") remains targeted for later in 2026/2027 **[Medium]**
