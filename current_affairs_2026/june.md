## 5. Defence: Exercises & Missile Tests

- **RudraM-II air-to-surface missile** — DRDO and IAF conducted a successful flight test on 2 June 2026, released from an airborne platform; a near-hypersonic missile with ~300 km range, developed by Research Centre Imarat (Hyderabad) with RF and IIR seekers for anti-radar and ground-attack roles **[High]**
- **Naval Anti-Ship Missile–Medium Range (NASM-MR)** — Maiden flight test conducted successfully; ~300 km range, midcourse INS+GPS guidance, X-band AESA RF seeker for terminal guidance, sea-skimming capability via radar altimeter **[High]**
- **AD-1 and AD-2 interceptors (BMD Phase-2)** — Three consecutive flight tests conducted for AD-1, AD-2, and NASM-MR on 10-11 June 2026; validated India's multi-layered Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) capability, including the ability to intercept even ICBM-class threats; AD-1 = endo-atmospheric/short-range, AD-2 = exo-atmospheric/longer-range; completion of BMD Phase-2 program **[High]**
- **BMD Phase-3** — Development now underway on AD-AH and AD-AM missiles, the next generation of India's ballistic missile defence **[Medium]**
- **Netra Airborne Early Warning and Control (AEW&C) system** — Received Final Operational Clearance (FOC) in June 2026, marking maturation of this indigenous surveillance/battle-management capability **[Medium]**
- **Long-Range Land-Attack Cruise Missile (LRLACM)** — Also successfully tested in June 2026 as part of the broader multi-missile test series **[Medium]**
