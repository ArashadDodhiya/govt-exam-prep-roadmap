# Medieval India — Delhi Sultanate to Maratha Empire

## 1. Overview
Medieval India spans the Delhi Sultanate, the Mughal Empire, the parallel southern Vijayanagara Empire, the Bhakti-Sufi socio-religious movements, and the rise of the Marathas. This period is a top scorer in exams because of its clear dynasty-wise structure, distinct rulers, and famous monuments/battles that are easy to convert into MCQs.

## 2. Core Facts (High-Yield)

**Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526 CE) — 5 Dynasties**
- Slave (Mamluk) Dynasty (1206–1290) — founded by Qutb-ud-din Aibak; started Qutub Minar (completed by Iltutmish) **[High]**
- Razia Sultana — only woman ruler of Delhi Sultanate, from Slave Dynasty **[High]**
- Khilji Dynasty (1290–1320) — Alauddin Khilji: market reforms, price control, defeated Mongols, conquered Deccan (via Malik Kafur) **[High]**
- Tughlaq Dynasty (1320–1414) — Muhammad bin Tughlaq: transferred capital to Daulatabad, token currency experiment (both failed) **[High]**
- Firoz Shah Tughlaq — canal building, welfare works, town of Firozabad **[Medium]**
- Sayyid Dynasty (1414–1451) and Lodi Dynasty (1451–1526) — Ibrahim Lodi defeated by Babur at First Battle of Panipat (1526) **[High]**
- Administration: Iqta system (land revenue assignment to nobles) **[Medium]**

**Mughal Empire (1526–1857 CE)**
- Babur — founder, won First Battle of Panipat (1526) against Ibrahim Lodi; also Battle of Khanwa (1527) vs Rana Sanga **[High]**
- Humayun — defeated by Sher Shah Suri, exiled, later regained throne **[Medium]**
- Sher Shah Suri (Suri Dynasty, interregnum) — Grand Trunk Road, currency reforms (Rupiya), land revenue system **[High]**
- Akbar — Battle of Panipat II (1556) vs Hemu; Din-i-Ilahi, Mansabdari system, Navratnas, married Rajput princesses **[High]**
- Jahangir — Nur Jahan's influence, Mughal painting flourished **[Medium]**
- Shah Jahan — Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Peacock Throne; "Golden Age" of Mughal architecture **[High]**
- Aurangzeb — last powerful Mughal, reversed religious tolerance, reimposed Jizya tax, empire overextended, Deccan wars **[High]**
- Decline after Aurangzeb (1707) — weak successors, regional powers rise (Marathas, Sikhs, Nawabs) **[Medium]**
- Mansabdari system — military-administrative ranking (zat and sawar) introduced by Akbar **[Medium]**

**Vijayanagara Empire (1336–1646 CE, South India)**
- Founded by Harihara and Bukka (brothers), guided by sage Vidyaranya **[High]**
- Krishnadeva Raya (Tuluva dynasty) — greatest ruler, patron of Telugu literature, Ashtadiggajas (8 poets), built temples **[High]**
- Capital: Hampi (Vijayanagara), major trade center **[High]**
- Battle of Talikota (1565) — Vijayanagara defeated by Deccan Sultanates confederacy, led to decline **[High]**
- Portuguese traveler Domingo Paes described the city's prosperity **[Low]**

**Bhakti and Sufi Movements**
- Bhakti Movement — devotion-based, rejected rituals/caste, emphasized personal connection to God **[High]**
- Key Bhakti saints: Kabir (Nirguna, syncretic), Guru Nanak (founded Sikhism), Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (Bengal, Krishna bhakti), Meera Bai (Krishna devotion), Ramananda, Tulsidas (Ramcharitmanas) **[High]**
- Alwars (Vishnu devotees) and Nayanars (Shiva devotees) — early South Indian Bhakti saints **[Medium]**
- Sufi Movement — Islamic mysticism, emphasized love, tolerance; organized into Silsilas (orders) **[High]**
- Major Sufi Silsilas: Chishti (most popular in India — Moinuddin Chishti, Ajmer), Suhrawardi, Qadiri, Naqshbandi **[High]**
- Nizamuddin Auliya — famous Chishti saint, Delhi **[Medium]**

**Maratha Empire (1674–1818 CE)**
- Founded by Shivaji Maharaji — coronated 1674 at Raigad, adopted title "Chhatrapati" **[High]**
- Shivaji's administration: Ashtapradhan (council of 8 ministers), guerrilla warfare tactics **[High]**
- Peshwa system — later Peshwas (esp. Baji Rao I) became de facto rulers, Maratha Confederacy expanded across India **[High]**
- Third Battle of Panipat (1761) — Marathas defeated by Ahmad Shah Abdali, major setback **[High]**
- Maratha Confederacy chiefs: Scindia (Gwalior), Holkar (Indore), Gaekwad (Baroda), Bhonsle (Nagpur) **[Medium]**
- Anglo-Maratha Wars (three, 1775–1818) ended with British annexation of Maratha territories **[Medium]**

## 4. Commonly Confused Facts

| Confusion | Distinguishing Rule |
|---|---|
| First vs Second vs Third Battle of Panipat | 1st (1526) = Babur founds Mughal Empire (beats Lodi); 2nd (1556) = Akbar secures throne (beats Hemu); 3rd (1761) = Marathas lose to Abdali |
| Alauddin Khilji vs Muhammad bin Tughlaq | Alauddin = market/price control reforms; Muhammad bin Tughlaq = capital transfer to Daulatabad + token currency (both his ideas failed) |
| Shivaji vs Peshwas | Shivaji = founder, coronated Chhatrapati 1674; Peshwas = later prime ministers who became the real power (esp. under weak later Chhatrapatis) |
| Bhakti vs Sufi Movement | Bhakti = Hindu devotional movement (rejects caste/ritual); Sufi = Islamic mystic movement (organized into Silsilas) — both stressed love & equality but from different religious roots |
| Alwars vs Nayanars | Alwars = devoted to Vishnu; Nayanars = devoted to Shiva — both South Indian, pre-date North Indian Bhakti saints |
| Akbar vs Aurangzeb religious policy | Akbar = tolerant, abolished Jizya, Din-i-Ilahi; Aurangzeb = orthodox, reimposed Jizya, destroyed some temples — opposite approaches |
| Razia Sultana's dynasty | Belongs to Slave (Mamluk) Dynasty, NOT Khilji — commonly misplaced |

## 5. Mnemonics / Memory Tricks

- **Delhi Sultanate dynasty order** — "**S**illy **K**ids **T**ry **S**inging **L**oudly" → **S**lave → **K**hilji → **T**ughlaq → **S**ayyid → **L**odi (chronological order of all 5 dynasties).
- **Mughal ruler sequence** — "**B**abur **H**ad **A**ll **J**ewelry **S**tolen **A**gain" → **B**abur → **H**umayun → **A**kbar → **J**ahangir → **S**hah Jahan → **A**urangzeb.
- **Panipat battles** — remember by RESULT tag: 1526="Empire Born" (Mughal), 1556="Empire Secured" (Akbar), 1761="Empire Broken" (Maratha loss) — link each date's last two digits to the outcome story.
- **Chishti Silsila popularity** — think "Chishti = Crowd-favorite" since it was the most popular and widely followed Sufi order in India, centered at Ajmer (Moinuddin Chishti's dargah, still visited today).
- **Maratha Confederacy chiefs** — "**S**cindia **H**olkar **G**aekwad **B**honsle" → picture 4 chiefs on 4 corners of a map: Gwalior (Scindia-North), Indore (Holkar-Central), Baroda (Gaekwad-West), Nagpur (Bhonsle-East).

## 6. Exam Trap Patterns

- Examiners frequently **swap the three Battles of Panipat** — mixing up which battle established, secured, or ended an empire.
- **"Founder" traps**: confusing who founded a dynasty vs who was its greatest/most famous ruler (e.g., Krishnadeva Raya is NOT the founder of Vijayanagara — Harihara & Bukka are).
- Bhakti saints get mixed with their **specific deity devotion** (Kabir = Nirguna/formless, Meera Bai = Krishna, Guru Nanak = founded a new faith) — options may swap these.
- "**Which of these reforms belongs to which ruler**" traps between Alauddin Khilji (market control) and Sher Shah Suri (currency/GT Road) — both are reform-famous but from different dynasties (Khilji vs Suri, not Mughal).

## 7. Quick Revision Summary
- Delhi Sultanate: Slave → Khilji → Tughlaq → Sayyid → Lodi; Razia Sultana (Slave), Alauddin Khilji (markets), Muhammad bin Tughlaq (failed capital shift), ends with Panipat I (1526).
- Mughals: Babur → Humayun → Akbar → Jahangir → Shah Jahan → Aurangzeb; Akbar=tolerant/Mansabdari, Shah Jahan=Taj Mahal, Aurangzeb=orthodox/decline begins.
- Vijayanagara: Harihara & Bukka founders, Krishnadeva Raya greatest, capital Hampi, fell after Talikota (1565).
- Bhakti (Hindu, Kabir/Nanak/Meera/Chaitanya) & Sufi (Islamic, Chishti most popular) — both devotional, anti-ritual movements.
- Marathas: Shivaji founder (1674, Chhatrapati) → Peshwas took power → lost Panipat III (1761) → Anglo-Maratha Wars → British annexation.

## 8. Self-Test (5 MCQs)

**Q1.** Who started the construction of Qutub Minar?
A) Iltutmish B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak C) Razia Sultana D) Balban

**Q2.** The Third Battle of Panipat (1761) was fought between the Marathas and:
A) Babur B) Nadir Shah C) Ahmad Shah Abdali D) Ibrahim Lodi

**Q3.** Which Sufi order (Silsila) was the most popular and widely followed in medieval India?
A) Suhrawardi B) Qadiri C) Naqshbandi D) Chishti

**Q4.** The Vijayanagara Empire suffered a major defeat leading to its decline in which battle?
A) Battle of Panipat B) Battle of Talikota C) Battle of Haldighati D) Battle of Plassey

**Q5.** Who among the following founded the Maratha Empire and was coronated as "Chhatrapati" in 1674?
A) Baji Rao I B) Shivaji Maharaj C) Sambhaji D) Balaji Vishwanath

## Answer Key
1. **B) Qutb-ud-din Aibak** — He began Qutub Minar's construction; Iltutmish completed it.
2. **C) Ahmad Shah Abdali** — The Marathas were defeated by Ahmad Shah Abdali in the Third Battle of Panipat (1761).
3. **D) Chishti** — The Chishti order, centered at Ajmer under Moinuddin Chishti, was the most popular Sufi Silsila in India.
4. **B) Battle of Talikota** — Fought in 1565 against a confederacy of Deccan Sultanates, causing Vijayanagara's decline.
5. **B) Shivaji Maharaj** — He founded the Maratha Empire and was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad in 1674.
