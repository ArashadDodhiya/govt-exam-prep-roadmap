# Ancient India — Indus Valley to Sangam Age

## 1. Overview
Ancient India covers the earliest civilizations (Harappan), the Vedic foundations of Indian society, the first pan-Indian empires (Mauryan, Gupta), and the parallel southern Sangam Age. Exams test this heavily because it's fact-dense (sites, rulers, dates) with lots of pairing opportunities (site–river, ruler–achievement) that make excellent MCQ distractors.

## 2. Core Facts (High-Yield)

**Indus Valley Civilization (IVC)**
- Bronze Age civilization, ~2500–1750 BCE, also called Harappan Civilization **[High]**
- Discovered 1921 (Harappa – Daya Ram Sahni) and 1922 (Mohenjo-daro – R.D. Banerji) **[High]**
- Script is pictographic and still undeciphered; written right to left **[High]**
- Town planning: grid pattern, Great Bath (Mohenjo-daro), granaries, drainage system **[High]**
- No evidence of temples, no clear evidence of horses; worshipped Mother Goddess, Pashupati seal **[High]**
- Economy based on trade (with Mesopotamia), agriculture, and craft (seals, beads) **[Medium]**
- Decline theories: Aryan invasion (older theory), climate change, river shifting (Ghaggar-Hakra drying), floods **[Medium]**
- Lothal (Gujarat) — known for dockyard **[High]**
- Kalibangan (Rajasthan) — ploughed field evidence, fire altars **[Medium]**
- Dholavira (Gujarat) — unique water conservation system, signboard **[Medium]**

**Vedic Period (~1500–600 BCE)**
- Early Vedic (Rigvedic, ~1500–1000 BCE) vs Later Vedic (~1000–600 BCE) **[High]**
- Four Vedas: Rig, Sama, Yajur, Atharva — Rigveda is oldest **[High]**
- Rigveda has 10 Mandalas, 1028 hymns (suktas); Gayatri Mantra in Mandala 3 **[Medium]**
- Vedangas (6 limbs of Vedas): Shiksha, Kalpa, Vyakarana, Nirukta, Chandas, Jyotisha **[Low]**
- Upanishads = Vedanta, philosophical texts, end of Vedic literature **[Medium]**
- Political bodies: Sabha and Samiti (early democratic-style assemblies) **[High]**
- Later Vedic period saw rise of varna system, iron use, larger kingdoms (Janapadas) **[High]**
- River Saraswati mentioned frequently in Rigveda; Indus (Sindhu) also central **[Medium]**

**Mauryan Empire (322–185 BCE)**
- Founded by Chandragupta Maurya (322 BCE), guided by Chanakya (Kautilya), defeated Nanda dynasty **[High]**
- Chandragupta defeated Seleucus Nicator (Greek); later abdicated, went to Shravanabelagola (Jainism) **[Medium]**
- Bindusara (son) — expanded empire southward, called "Amitraghata" **[Medium]**
- Ashoka the Great (273–232 BCE) — grandson; Kalinga War (261 BCE) turned him to Buddhism **[High]**
- Ashoka's edicts: written in Brahmi (most), Kharoshthi (NW India), Greek-Aramaic (Afghanistan) **[High]**
- Ashokan pillars — Sarnath Lion Capital is India's National Emblem **[High]**
- Arthashastra (by Kautilya) — treatise on statecraft, economics, military strategy **[High]**
- Megasthenes (Greek ambassador) wrote "Indica" describing Mauryan administration **[Medium]**
- Administration: centralized, divided into provinces under princes; spies (secret service) used **[Low]**

**Gupta Empire (320–550 CE) — "Golden Age"**
- Founded by Chandragupta I (320 CE); adopted title "Maharajadhiraja" **[High]**
- Samudragupta — called "Napoleon of India," great conqueror, Prayaga Prashasti (Allahabad Pillar Inscription) by Harisena **[High]**
- Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya) — defeated the Shakas, patronized Navratnas (9 gems) including Kalidasa **[High]**
- Fa-Hien (Chinese traveler) visited during Chandragupta II's reign **[High]**
- Golden Age achievements: Aryabhata (zero, astronomy), Nalanda University roots, Ajanta cave paintings, Kalidasa's works (Abhijnanashakuntalam) **[High]**
- Decline due to Huna (Hun) invasions **[Medium]**

**Sangam Age (~300 BCE–300 CE, South India)**
- Three ruling dynasties: Chera, Chola, Pandya (parallel to Mauryan/post-Mauryan north) **[High]**
- Sangam literature — Tamil poetic works, compiled in three Sangams (assemblies) held under Pandya patronage in Madurai **[Medium]**
- Chera — Kerala region, capital Vanji; famous for pepper trade with Rome **[Medium]**
- Chola — Kaveri delta, capital Uraiyur, later Puhar (major port) **[Medium]**
- Pandya — capital Madurai, pearl fisheries, Sangam patrons **[Medium]**
- Extensive Roman trade — Roman gold coins found at Arikamedu (Tamil Nadu) **[Medium]**
- Tolkappiyam — oldest Tamil grammar text of Sangam period **[Low]**

## 4. Commonly Confused Facts

| Confusion | Distinguishing Rule |
|---|---|
| Harappa vs Mohenjo-daro | Harappa discovered FIRST (1921, Punjab, Ravi river); Mohenjo-daro is LARGER and has the Great Bath (Sindh, Indus river) |
| Chandragupta Maurya vs Chandragupta I vs Chandragupta II | Maurya = founder of Mauryan Empire (322 BCE); Chandragupta I = founder of Gupta Empire (320 CE); Chandragupta II = Gupta king with title "Vikramaditya," defeated Shakas |
| Ashoka's edict scripts | Brahmi = most edicts (read left to right); Kharoshthi = only NW India (read right to left); Greek-Aramaic = Afghanistan region only |
| Samudragupta vs Chandragupta II achievements | Samudragupta = military conqueror ("Napoleon of India"); Chandragupta II = cultural peak, Navratnas, defeated Shakas specifically |
| Fa-Hien vs Hiuen Tsang | Fa-Hien visited during Chandragupta II (Gupta period); Hiuen Tsang visited later, during Harshavardhana's reign (NOT Gupta) |
| Vedas order | Rigveda = oldest (hymns); Samaveda = music/chants; Yajurveda = rituals/sacrifice formulas; Atharvaveda = spells/magic, youngest |
| Sabha vs Samiti | Sabha = council of elders/select members; Samiti = general assembly of people — both existed in Rigvedic period |

## 5. Mnemonics / Memory Tricks

- **Four Vedas order** — "RSYA" → **R**ig, **S**ama, **Y**ajur, **A**tharva (Rig is oldest, Atharva is youngest). Say it as "**R**ats **S**ing **Y**ellow **A**nthems."
- **Mauryan ruler sequence** — "Cha-Bi-Ashoka" → **Cha**ndragupta → **Bi**ndusara → **Ashoka** (grandfather → father → grandson).
- **Gupta ruler sequence** — "C1-S-C2" → **C**handragupta **I** → **S**amudragupta → **C**handragupta **II** (three generations, each bigger than the last).
- **Sangam dynasties** — "Please Choose Chera" → **P**andya, **C**hola, **C**hera — or simply remember **CCP** (like the initials), all three ruled Tamil regions simultaneously, not sequentially.
- **IVC site–river pairing** — picture each site sitting ON its river bank: Harappa-Ravi, Mohenjo-daro-Indus, Lothal-Bhogava, Kalibangan-Ghaggar. Visualize a mini-map instead of a list.

## 6. Exam Trap Patterns

- Examiners swap **site and discoverer years** (e.g., saying Mohenjo-daro was discovered in 1921 instead of 1922) — always double check which site came first.
- **Achievement swapping** between Samudragupta and Chandragupta II (military conquest vs Shaka defeat/cultural patronage) is a very common trap.
- "**Which of the following is NOT correct**" questions often hide one wrong script-region pairing among Ashoka's edicts (Brahmi/Kharoshthi/Greek-Aramaic).
- Confusing **Fa-Hien (Gupta period)** with **Hiuen Tsang (Harshavardhana's period, post-Gupta)** — these belong to different centuries and different kings entirely.

## 7. Quick Revision Summary
- IVC: Bronze Age, ~2500 BCE, undeciphered script, Harappa (1921) & Mohenjo-daro (1922), no temples/horses, Great Bath, Lothal=dockyard, Dholavira=water system.
- Vedic: 4 Vedas (Rig oldest), Sabha & Samiti, Later Vedic = iron + varna system.
- Mauryan: Chandragupta Maurya (Chanakya) → Bindusara → Ashoka (Kalinga War → Buddhism), Arthashastra, Brahmi/Kharoshthi/Greek-Aramaic edicts.
- Gupta: Golden Age, Chandragupta I (founder) → Samudragupta (conqueror) → Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya, Navratnas, Fa-Hien visited).
- Sangam: Chera-Chola-Pandya, Tamil literature, Roman trade (Arikamedu).

## 8. Self-Test (5 MCQs)

**Q1.** Which site of the Indus Valley Civilization is famous for its dockyard?
A) Harappa B) Mohenjo-daro C) Lothal D) Kalibangan

**Q2.** The Gayatri Mantra is found in which Mandala of the Rigveda?
A) Mandala 1 B) Mandala 3 C) Mandala 7 D) Mandala 10

**Q3.** Which script was used in Ashoka's edicts found in the north-western region of the empire?
A) Brahmi B) Devanagari C) Kharoshthi D) Pali

**Q4.** Who among the following defeated the Shakas and adopted the title "Vikramaditya"?
A) Samudragupta B) Chandragupta I C) Chandragupta II D) Kumaragupta

**Q5.** The three Tamil dynasties of the Sangam Age were:
A) Chera, Chalukya, Pandya
B) Chera, Chola, Pandya
C) Chola, Pallava, Pandya
D) Chera, Chola, Pallava

## Answer Key
1. **C) Lothal** — Lothal (Gujarat) is specifically known for its ancient dockyard, unique among IVC sites.
2. **B) Mandala 3** — The Gayatri Mantra appears in Mandala 3 of the Rigveda, attributed to sage Vishwamitra.
3. **C) Kharoshthi** — Kharoshthi (read right to left) was used only in the north-western edicts; Brahmi was used elsewhere.
4. **C) Chandragupta II** — He defeated the Shakas and took the title "Vikramaditya"; Samudragupta is known for conquest, not the Shaka defeat.
5. **B) Chera, Chola, Pandya** — These three dynasties ruled parallel Tamil kingdoms during the Sangam Age.
