# Indian Polity — Union Government (President, PM, Parliament)

## 1. Overview
The Union Government topic covers India's central executive (President, Vice-President, PM, Council of Ministers) and legislature (Parliament — Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha, Speaker, legislative process). This is a high-frequency exam topic because of its clear structure, defined tenures/qualifications, and step-by-step processes that are easy to test as sequencing or "who does what" questions.

## 2. Core Facts (High-Yield)

**President of India**
- Head of State (nominal/constitutional head); executive power vested in President but exercised via Council of Ministers (Art 53) **[High]**
- Elected indirectly by an Electoral College: elected members of Parliament (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha) + elected members of State Legislative Assemblies (and UTs of Delhi & Puducherry) **[High]**
- Election method: Proportional Representation by means of Single Transferable Vote, via secret ballot **[Medium]**
- Term: 5 years; eligible for re-election (no limit on number of terms) **[High]**
- Minimum age: 35 years; must be a citizen of India, qualified to be a Lok Sabha member **[Medium]**
- Can be removed only by IMPEACHMENT for "violation of the Constitution" — initiated in EITHER House, needs 2/3 majority in both Houses **[High]**
- Key powers: appoints PM, Governors, judges of SC/HC; can grant pardon (Art 72) — pardon, reprieve, respite, remission, commutation; Ordinance-making power (Art 123) when Parliament not in session **[High]**
- Dr. Rajendra Prasad — first President of India (also only President to serve two terms) **[High]**
- Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam — 11th President, known as "People's President," scientist background **[Medium]**
- Droupadi Murmu — first tribal woman President of India **[High]**

**Vice-President of India**
- Ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha (Art 64) **[High]**
- Elected by an Electoral College consisting of members (elected + nominated) of BOTH Houses of Parliament (unlike President's college, no state legislature involvement) **[High]**
- Term: 5 years; acts as President if a vacancy occurs (until new President elected, max 6 months) **[Medium]**
- Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan — first Vice-President of India (later became 2nd President) **[High]**
- Removal: by a resolution of Rajya Sabha (passed by majority) AND agreed to by Lok Sabha — no impeachment process like the President **[Medium]**

**Prime Minister & Council of Ministers**
- Real executive head of government; appointed by the President (usually leader of majority party/coalition in Lok Sabha) **[High]**
- Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the LOK SABHA (Art 75) **[High]**
- Three ranks: Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State (independent charge or otherwise), Deputy Ministers **[Medium]**
- PM need not be a member of Parliament at time of appointment but must become one (either House) within 6 months **[Medium]**
- Jawaharlal Nehru — first Prime Minister of India (longest-serving PM) **[High]**
- Cabinet — smaller, inner decision-making body within the Council of Ministers **[Medium]**

**Parliament — Lok Sabha (House of the People)**
- Lower House; directly elected by citizens via universal adult suffrage **[High]**
- Maximum strength: 552 (currently around 543 elected + can include nominated, per amendments — composition periodically revised) **[Medium]**
- Term: 5 years, can be dissolved earlier; can be extended during a National Emergency **[High]**
- Minimum age to be a member: 25 years **[High]**
- Money Bills can ONLY originate in Lok Sabha (Art 110) **[High]**
- Speaker presides over Lok Sabha, elected by its own members **[High]**

**Parliament — Rajya Sabha (Council of States)**
- Upper House; represents STATES; members elected by elected members of State Legislative Assemblies (indirect election) **[High]**
- PERMANENT body — NOT subject to dissolution; 1/3rd of members retire every 2 years (6-year term per member) **[High]**
- Maximum strength: 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated by President for expertise in art, literature, science, social service) **[High]**
- Minimum age to be a member: 30 years **[High]**
- Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman; Deputy Chairman elected from among members **[Medium]**
- Rajya Sabha CANNOT reject/amend a Money Bill — can only recommend changes within 14 days, Lok Sabha's decision is final **[High]**

**Speaker of Lok Sabha**
- Presiding officer, elected from among Lok Sabha members; decides on Money Bill certification **[High]**
- Casts a vote only in case of a tie (casting vote) **[Medium]**
- G.V. Mavalankar — first Speaker of independent India's Lok Sabha **[Medium]**
- Deputy Speaker also elected from among members, presides in Speaker's absence **[Low]**

**Legislative Process (How a Bill becomes Law)**
- Types of Bills: Ordinary Bill, Money Bill (Art 110, needs Speaker's certification), Financial Bill, Constitutional Amendment Bill **[High]**
- Ordinary Bill process: introduced in either House → 3 readings (First, Second, Third) → passed by both Houses → President's assent **[High]**
- Money Bill: introduced ONLY in Lok Sabha, President's prior recommendation needed; Rajya Sabha has only 14 days to return with recommendations (non-binding) **[High]**
- President's assent options: give assent, withhold assent, OR return the bill (except Money Bills) for reconsideration once — if repassed, must give assent **[Medium]**
- Joint Sitting of Parliament (Art 108) — called by President in case of deadlock between the two Houses on an Ordinary Bill; presided over by the Speaker of Lok Sabha; NOT applicable to Money Bills **[High]**

## 4. Commonly Confused Facts

| Confusion | Distinguishing Rule |
|---|---|
| President's Electoral College vs Vice-President's Electoral College | President = MPs (elected) + MLAs of states/UTs (Delhi, Puducherry); Vice-President = MPs (elected + nominated) of BOTH Houses ONLY — no MLAs involved |
| President removal vs Vice-President removal | President = formal IMPEACHMENT (2/3 majority in both Houses, either House can initiate); Vice-President = simple Rajya Sabha resolution + Lok Sabha agreement, no impeachment |
| Money Bill vs Ordinary Bill (origination) | Money Bill = ONLY Lok Sabha; Ordinary Bill = EITHER House |
| Money Bill vs Ordinary Bill (Rajya Sabha's power) | Money Bill = Rajya Sabha can only delay 14 days, no amendment power; Ordinary Bill = Rajya Sabha has full power to amend/reject, deadlock resolved via Joint Sitting |
| Joint Sitting applicability | Applies to Ordinary Bills & Financial Bills in deadlock — does NOT apply to Money Bills or Constitutional Amendment Bills |
| First President vs first PM vs first VP | Rajendra Prasad = first PRESIDENT; Nehru = first PRIME MINISTER; Radhakrishnan = first VICE-PRESIDENT — three different "firsts," commonly mixed |
| Cabinet vs Council of Ministers | Council of Ministers = ALL ministers (Cabinet + Ministers of State + Deputy Ministers); Cabinet = smaller INNER group of top/senior ministers only |

## 5. Mnemonics / Memory Tricks

- **President's powers acronym** — "**A**ppoints, **P**ardons, **O**rdinance" → **A**ppoints PM/Governors/Judges, **P**ardoning power (Art 72), **O**rdinance-making (Art 123) — three pillars of presidential power to recall quickly.
- **Rajya Sabha permanence** — think "Raj-YA Sabha = stays, like a permanent RAJ (kingdom)" — never dissolved, unlike Lok Sabha which can fall with elections.
- **Money Bill origin rule** — "Money only grows in the LOK (people's) garden" — Money Bills can only be introduced in Lok Sabha, since it directly represents taxpayers.
- **First-holders trio** — "**R**ajendra=**P**resident, **N**ehru=**PM**, **R**adhakrishnan=**VP**" — remember as three separate "R-N-R" first-holders so they don't blur together.
- **14-day Rajya Sabha Money Bill window** — "14 days = 2 weeks to just SUGGEST, not decide" — a short, memorable cap on Rajya Sabha's limited role in Money Bills.

## 6. Exam Trap Patterns

- Examiners frequently swap **President's vs Vice-President's electoral college composition** — the presence/absence of MLAs is the key differentiator to check.
- **Removal process traps**: assuming Vice-President is impeached like the President — VP removal is a simpler Rajya Sabha resolution process, NOT impeachment.
- Confusing whether **Joint Sitting applies to Money Bills** — it does NOT; Money Bill disputes are resolved differently (Lok Sabha's decision is final after the 14-day Rajya Sabha window).
- Swapping **Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha's dissolution status** — Rajya Sabha is a PERMANENT body, a fact often tested as a standalone "which house cannot be dissolved" question.
- Age-requirement mix-ups between Lok Sabha (25 years) and Rajya Sabha (30 years) membership eligibility.

## 7. Quick Revision Summary
- President: 5-yr term, elected by MPs+MLAs, impeachment removal, appoints PM/Governors/Judges, pardon & ordinance powers; Rajendra Prasad=first.
- Vice-President: ex-officio Rajya Sabha Chairman, elected by MPs only, simpler removal process; Radhakrishnan=first.
- PM & Council of Ministers: real executive, responsible to Lok Sabha; Nehru=first PM.
- Lok Sabha: lower house, directly elected, 5-yr term, can dissolve, Money Bills originate here only.
- Rajya Sabha: upper house, indirectly elected (state MLAs), permanent, 1/3 retire every 2 yrs, limited power over Money Bills (14-day window).
- Legislative process: Ordinary Bill (either House, 3 readings, President's assent) vs Money Bill (Lok Sabha only, Speaker's certification, Rajya Sabha can't block); Joint Sitting resolves Ordinary Bill deadlocks only.

## 8. Self-Test (5 MCQs)

**Q1.** The President of India is elected by an Electoral College consisting of:
A) Only Lok Sabha members
B) Elected members of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies
C) All citizens of India directly
D) Only Rajya Sabha members

**Q2.** Who was the first Vice-President of India?
A) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
B) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan
C) Zakir Husain
D) V.V. Giri

**Q3.** A Money Bill can be introduced only in which House of Parliament?
A) Rajya Sabha B) Either House C) Lok Sabha D) Joint Sitting

**Q4.** Which of the following Houses of Parliament is a permanent body and cannot be dissolved?
A) Lok Sabha B) Rajya Sabha C) Both D) Neither

**Q5.** A Joint Sitting of Parliament (Article 108) is called to resolve a deadlock on which type of Bill?
A) Money Bill B) Constitutional Amendment Bill C) Ordinary Bill D) Finance Bill only

## Answer Key
1. **B) Elected members of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies** — The President's Electoral College includes elected MPs and elected MLAs (including Delhi and Puducherry).
2. **B) Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan** — He served as India's first Vice-President before later becoming the second President.
3. **C) Lok Sabha** — Article 110 mandates that Money Bills can only be introduced in the Lok Sabha, with prior Presidential recommendation.
4. **B) Rajya Sabha** — It is a permanent body; only one-third of its members retire every two years, unlike the Lok Sabha which can be dissolved.
5. **C) Ordinary Bill** — Joint Sittings resolve deadlocks specifically on Ordinary Bills (and some Financial Bills), not Money Bills.
